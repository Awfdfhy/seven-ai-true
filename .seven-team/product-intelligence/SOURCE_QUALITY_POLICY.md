# Product Intelligence Source Quality Policy

## Source priority
1. First-party product docs/changelogs/design posts with current screenshots or explicit behavior.
2. Official app/store pages.
3. Reputable design/engineering write-ups with reproducible examples.
4. High-signal user reports for pain points only.
5. Unverified social posts only as leads, never as truth.

## Rules
- Record source URL, product, date observed, and exact lesson.
- Separate observed UI behavior from inferred design intent.
- Prefer recent sources when the product changes quickly.
- Do not treat marketing claims as runtime proof.
- Do not copy proprietary assets or layouts.
- Community consensus can identify friction, but must not override direct evidence.
- A visual reference is not a target screenshot; it is evidence for principles.
- Every learned entry must say what Seven should adopt, avoid, or test.
