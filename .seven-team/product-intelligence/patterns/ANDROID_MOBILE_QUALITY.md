Give immediate launch feedback; keep input responsive; target smooth rendering; use platform profiling and StrictMode-style thinking for blocking work.

## State

Home/recents, background/foreground, rotation, configuration change and process recreation must preserve committed user state and active drafts as defined by product contract.

## Device matrix

At least one small phone, current target API, representative older supported API, font scaling, RTL, dark/light, gesture navigation and keyboard-open states.
