# Pattern Library — Typography, Spacing, Color, Icons & Motion

Status: ACTIVE KNOWLEDGE PACK

## Typography

Small, intentional type scale; body readability first; metadata visibly secondary; code/prose distinction; Arabic-specific line-height/truncation review.

## Spacing

Use semantic spacing tokens and consistent vertical rhythm. Group related controls through proximity rather than extra borders.

## Color

Semantic roles: background/surface/text/secondary/accent/error/warning/success/selection/focus. Day/night are independent validated themes.

## Icons

Use one family/grammar; label ambiguous actions; mirror only semantically directional icons in RTL.

## Motion

Motion explains continuity and state. Prefer short, interruptible transitions; no animation polling loops; respect reduced motion; heavy work may downgrade effects.
