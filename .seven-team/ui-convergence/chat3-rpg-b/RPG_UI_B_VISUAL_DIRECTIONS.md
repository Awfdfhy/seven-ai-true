Scale: 1 weak — 5 strong.

| Direction | Pros | Cons | Density | Mobile | RTL | Seven identity |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tactical Minimal | fastest gameplay reading; excellent contextual battle/action UI | can feel dry without Seven accents | low-medium | 5 | 5 | 4 |

| Modern Fantasy HUD | flexible combat + inventory language | easy to drift into generic game HUD | medium | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Living World Dashboard | ideal for world pulse/events/factions | highest dashboard-syndrome risk | high if unbounded | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| Clean JRPG Mobile | excellent phone hierarchy and touchability | can look too “mobile game” if over-carded | medium | 5 | 4 | 4 |

## Recommended synthesis for Chat 0
**Canonical core: Tactical Minimal + Royal Strategy + Living World (contextual only).**

Use:
- Tactical Minimal for battle/actions/status.
- Royal Strategy for faction/kingdom/diplomacy.
- Living World for maps/events/world pulse.
- Clean JRPG Mobile only as a responsive layout discipline.
- Arcane Systems only as a restrained accent for real ability/progression graphs.
- Modern Fantasy HUD only where a short-lived combat context strip is justified.

This combination scores highest because it keeps story immersion while making the simulation legible.
