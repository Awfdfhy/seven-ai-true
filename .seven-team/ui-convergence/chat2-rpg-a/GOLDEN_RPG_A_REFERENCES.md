# Golden RPG A References

These are **pattern references**, not visual templates to copy.

## G1 — Disco Elysium — Dialogue / inner voices
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/15554120221115396

Extract:
- prose-first vertical rhythm
- speaker/source identity embedded into reading flow
- choices remain distinct without turning the page into a dashboard

Do not copy:
- exact typography, portrait treatment, colored skill voices, or column geometry.

Seven use:
- narrator/character differentiation and long-form readability.

## G2 — Pentiment — Contextual dialogue
Source: https://www.neoseeker.com/pentiment/walkthrough/Act_I

Extract:
- dialogue identity that feels native to the world
- options attached to scene context
- personality through typography/structure rather than constant stats

Do not copy:
- manuscript visual language or hand cursor.

Seven use:
- scene-aware choices and strong identity with minimal persistent chrome.

## G3 — Persona 5 Royal — Confidant / choice hierarchy
Source: https://megatenwiki.com/wiki/Category:Persona_5_Royal_Screenshots

Extract:
- unmistakable identity hierarchy
- relationship content separated from ordinary navigation
- choices are visually decisive and scannable

Do not copy:
- signature angular panels, exact black/red/white palette, font, portrait silhouettes.

Seven use:
- relationship focus and choice emphasis, heavily reduced for accessibility/mobile.

## G4 — Fire Emblem: Three Houses — Support relationships
Source: https://samurai-gamers.com/fire-emblem-three-houses/menu-guide/

Extract:
- relationship state is inspectable across a cast
- overview first, pair detail second

Do not copy:
- letter-rank progression or exact support grid.

Seven use:
- cast/relationship overview with multidimensional Seven state rather than one rank.

## G5 — Mass Effect — Codex
Source: https://www.gamereactor.es/mass-effect-datapad-ya-en-ios/

Extract:
- strong taxonomy
- focused reading pane
- hierarchy separates primary from secondary knowledge

Do not copy:
- sci-fi frame language or exact category tree.

Seven use:
- lore/canon archive information architecture.

## G6 — The Witcher 3 — Quest journal
Source: https://www.playground.ru/witcher_3_wild_hunt/file/witcher_3_ispravlenie_zhurnala_zadanij-1097657

Extract:
- active/completed segmentation
- objective focus with contextual metadata
- quest list and detail remain distinct

Do not copy:
- category names, heraldry, level UI or exact two-pane layout.

Seven use:
- journal filter logic; mobile converts list/detail to sequential screens.

## Golden synthesis
**Story Chat:** Disco Elysium + Pentiment  
**Relationships:** Fire Emblem + Persona principles, but Seven's multidimensional runtime  
**Archive:** Mass Effect + Witcher information architecture  
**Seven identity:** Modern AI × Fantasy semantic layer, not a copy of any franchise.
