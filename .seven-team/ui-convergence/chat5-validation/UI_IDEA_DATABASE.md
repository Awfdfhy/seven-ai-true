- Prefer story-first stream, contextual panels and bottom sheets over permanent HUD.
- Use Ancient Codex selectively for archive/canon; do not skin the whole product as a manuscript.
- Use Tactical Kingdom only when world/faction state is actually available.
- Avoid split-pane as a mobile default.
- Keep Modern AI × Fantasy as the bridge between Core Seven and RPG identity.
- Reject interaction novelty that does not improve clarity, touch reach, or narrative flow.
