# GOLDEN_REFERENCES — Principle Shortlist v0

This is an initial principle shortlist, not the completed Top 100 → Top 30 → Golden 12 closure.

1. Material 3 — adaptive mobile components, sheets/dialogs, touch geometry.
2. Apple HIG — menus, context actions, sheets and transient hierarchy.
3. Fluent 2 — spacing discipline, accessibility and 320px responsive reflow.
4. Dark Fantasy RPG UI System (Figma Community showcase) — cohesive inventory/abilities/quests/dialogue/HUD grammar.
5. The Wolf Among Us — cinematic dialogue choices with low permanent HUD footprint.
6. Throne and Liberty Codex — objective/codex hierarchy.
7. Modern AI chat patterns — quiet composer-first conversation.
8. Notion-class productivity patterns — progressive disclosure and dense-but-scannable information.
9. Discord-class navigation — workspace/channel mental models adapted cautiously.
10. Persona-style relationship/status presentation — expressive identity with clear hierarchy.
11. Tactical kingdom interfaces — faction/world-state overview.
12. Visual novel interfaces — speaker identity, pacing and history/log access.

Golden status requires a transferable principle, mobile/RTL viability and no cloning dependence.
