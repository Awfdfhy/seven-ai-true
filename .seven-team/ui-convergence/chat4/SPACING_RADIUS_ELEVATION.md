# Spacing, Radius, Elevation

Spacing scale: 2/4/8/12/16/20/24/32/40. Radius: 8 control-small, 12 control/input, 16 card, 20 dialog, 24 sheet, 999 pill. Elevation: none/subtle/menu/dialog/critical. Components may compose these tokens but may not invent 13/17/22px spacing or 13/14/15/18/26px radii. Existing exceptions must be migrated or explicitly documented.
