## Why the 2/2 freeze is legitimately reopened

The previous saturation result remains valid for the scope that it tested. It is not deleted or falsified. However, a new high-value domain was identified after freeze:

**Character visual identity and visual continuity were not modeled at parity with psychological identity, perspective, causality and world continuity.**

This is large enough to affect:
- character recognition and immersion
- Real Works canon fidelity
- transformations and outfit continuity
- disguise and mistaken identity
- images, portraits, storyboards and future video generation
- visual scene continuity across long campaigns
- player-created character persistence
- multimodal perception
- visual evidence/provenance

Therefore the old saturation counter is reset for Capability 19.

Previous counter: `2 / 2`
New counter: **`0 / 2`**
