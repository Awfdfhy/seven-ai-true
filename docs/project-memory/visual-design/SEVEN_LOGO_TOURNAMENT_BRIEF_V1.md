# Seven Logo & Identity Tournament Brief 1.0

Status: PHASE_3_TOURNAMENT_BRIEF
Governance: Visual Intelligence V1.4 + VISUAL-EPOCH-1

## Objective
Find a master Seven mark that survives app identity, Android launcher masking, monochrome theming, tiny sizes, Day/Night, splash usage and motion without becoming generic or over-detailed.

The tournament selects geometry first. Color/effects cannot rescue a weak silhouette.

## Non-negotiable requirements
- recognizable silhouette
- survives 24-32px practical small display
- clean monochrome form
- Android adaptive foreground/background compatibility
- safe inside the 66x66dp guaranteed central region of a 108x108dp adaptive layer
- works against light and dark backgrounds
- no dependence on tiny internal detail
- no required text
- no baked launcher mask
- no background shadow baked into adaptive layer outline
- vector-friendly geometry
- low asset/render cost
- no suspicious imitation of a known product identity
- same underlying brand family in Day and Night

## Candidate families

### L-A: Seven + Continuity
A geometric 7 fused or interlocked with a continuity/infinity gesture.
Goal: express Seven + persistent intelligence without writing two separate symbols.
Primary risk: infinity marks are common; the Seven geometry must make it unmistakably proprietary in character.

### L-B: Celestial Seven
One master Seven/continuity geometry with controlled Day/Night celestial treatments.
Day exploration may use solar/radiant structure; Night exploration may use lunar/orbital structure while preserving the same core silhouette.
Goal: create memorable Day/Night identity without two unrelated logos.
Primary risk: avoid fantasy-app or weather-app appearance.

### L-C: Orbital Seven
A compact 7 intersected by one orbital path/node.
Goal: connect cognition, runtime state and the Orbit Thread of the UI genome.
Primary risk: orbit motifs are common; geometry must remain distinctive and readable at tiny sizes.

### L-D: Folded Seven
A single folded/ribbon-like 7 whose negative space implies continuity.
Goal: maximum silhouette strength and vector simplicity.
Primary risk: ribbon marks can become corporate/generic.

### L-E: Pure Seven Glyph
Reduce the idea to the smallest proprietary geometric mark possible.
Goal: strongest monochrome/tiny-size performance and long-term durability.
Primary risk: may lose the richer Seven story unless motion/theme treatment restores character.

### L-F: Wildcard
A direction generated from first-principles brand goals without requiring literal 7, infinity, sun, moon or orbit motifs.
Goal: prevent the tournament from overfitting our initial symbolism.
Primary risk: conceptual distance from the name Seven.

## Candidate generation
Seed several structurally diverse candidates per family, then prune dominated variants early. Do not satisfy a fixed quantity if new candidates add no real diversity.

Keep baseline/current mark as a control.

## Evaluation stages

### Stage 1: silhouette kill test
Black/white only. Reject weak, generic or detail-dependent geometry.

### Stage 2: tiny-size test
16/24/32/48/64px previews. The core symbol must remain identifiable even if fine detail disappears.

### Stage 3: adaptive mask test
108x108dp layers, central safe region, circle/squircle/rounded-square/aggressive mask previews.

### Stage 4: monochrome/themed test
One-color geometry under multiple system tints.

### Stage 5: Day/Night treatment
Prove theme personality without changing the core brand into a second logo.

### Stage 6: product-context test
Launcher, splash, sidebar, empty state, top bar, settings/about, loading/brand moment.

### Stage 7: motion-mark test
Simple launch/orbit/cut/settle possibilities. Motion cannot be required for recognition.

### Stage 8: distinctiveness review
Bounded comparison against relevant major app/software/AI identity landscape. Flag close overall impression or geometry.

### Stage 9: human recognition/preference evidence
Where available, compare recognition, recall, concept fit and preference. Human votes cannot override functional/accessibility constraints.

### Stage 10: final simplifier attack
Ask what can be removed while preserving identity. If a simpler candidate preserves all value, the heavier candidate loses.

## Scoring discipline
Do not collapse the tournament into one opaque number.

Preserve separate judgments for:
- silhouette
- small-size survival
- adaptive-mask resilience
- monochrome survival
- distinctiveness
- Seven concept fit
- Day/Night fit
- motion potential
- implementation simplicity
- asset cost
- long-term durability

## Promotion verdicts
- FINALIST
- REJECT
- DISTINCTIVENESS_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- TRADEOFF_REQUIRES_DECISION
- INCONCLUSIVE

Final brand freeze occurs only after the winning geometry is exported and the Android/app pipeline is proven to consume the exact approved assets.

## Truth boundary
This brief intentionally does not declare a winning mark. The current symbolic preferences are candidate directions, not evidence that one family is strongest.
