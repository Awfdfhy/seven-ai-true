# Seven Visual Evaluation Constitution 1.0

Status: PHASE_1_LOCK_CANDIDATE
Parent: SEVEN_VISUAL_INTELLIGENCE_AND_DESIGN_FABRIC_V1.md
Comparison epoch: VISUAL-EPOCH-1

## 1. Purpose
Freeze the rules that decide whether a visual candidate is better before candidate generation begins.

A candidate is not promoted because it is newer, more fashionable or preferred by one evaluator. It must survive the same locked evidence protocol as the baseline.

## 2. Protected invariants

C0 noncompensable visual invariants:
- no false semantic state or fake success/verification
- no regression of authoritative runtime meaning
- no inaccessible critical action introduced by visual treatment
- no functional control lost or hidden by redesign
- no release/source integrity violation
- no unapproved modification of seven_ai-final.html
- no broken Arabic/RTL or catastrophic text clipping in required scenarios
- no loss of cancellation/error/warning visibility
- no hidden permission or side-effect boundary
- no material Lite-tier failure on required mobile scenarios

C1 protected product invariants:
- Day/Night meaning parity
- Reduced Motion equivalence
- effective touch reachability
- keyboard/IME reachability
- workspace identity remains coherent with global Seven identity
- domain personality does not change core interaction vocabulary unpredictably
- lazy loading and mobile resource discipline remain intact

C0 failures reject immediately. C1 failures reject unless the constitution explicitly classifies a documented exception before evaluation.

## 3. Evidence channels

Every material candidate uses multiple evidence channels.

A. Structural / deterministic
- DOM/layout bounds
- CSS/token values
- contrast calculations
- hit-target geometry
- overflow/clipping
- semantic attributes where observable
- theme/state token mappings
- safe-area behavior
- reduced-motion switches
- asset geometry and bytes

B. Functional replay
- task completion
- navigation
- composer/input
- model/tool/research/workspace controls
- errors/recovery
- permissions
- orientation/window/keyboard scenarios

C. Render evidence
- screenshots
- frame sequences
- screen recordings
- mask previews for icons

D. Runtime performance evidence
- startup work/bytes
- long tasks
- event responsiveness where measurable
- frame pacing during important motion
- memory/resource traces where available
- hidden/offscreen activity

E. Independent visual judgment
- hierarchy
- readability
- density
- coherence
- distinctiveness
- brand fit
- emotional tone
- motion quality
- domain personality

F. User/device evidence later
- representative Android devices
- accessibility services
- real font scaling
- OEM launcher masks/theming
- battery/thermal observation where material

No single channel may silently stand in for another.

## 4. Objective accessibility targets

Seven's target is stronger than the minimum web floor where practical.

### Touch
- Ordinary mobile interactive controls target an effective 48x48dp hit region.
- The visible icon/glyph may be smaller if the interactive hit region is verified.
- Exceptions must be documented and must still satisfy applicable WCAG 2.2 target-size rules.

### Contrast
- Normal text target: at least 4.5:1 unless an applicable WCAG exception exists.
- Large text target: at least 3:1.
- Meaningful non-text UI/focus boundaries target at least 3:1 where applicable.
- Seven does not rely on color alone for warning/error/verified/uncertain semantic distinction.

### Motion
- Nonessential interaction-triggered motion has a Reduced Motion path.
- Reduced Motion may reduce movement but must preserve state meaning and task feedback.
- No unsafe rapid flashing patterns.

### Text/reflow
Required scenarios must test larger text, long labels, Arabic, English and mixed bidi without loss of critical controls or information.

## 5. Android identity targets

Launcher candidates must be evaluated as a system:
- 108x108dp adaptive foreground/background layers
- central 66x66dp mask-safe region
- foreground mark within Android's documented practical bounds
- monochrome/themed-icon layer
- multiple mask previews
- small-size previews
- light/dark launcher backgrounds where relevant
- no baked mask or edge shadow that conflicts with adaptive masking

Splash identity is evaluated separately from launcher identity; neither is allowed to force heavy startup work.

## 6. Performance constitution

Beauty cannot purchase unbounded work.

Until representative-device absolute budgets are measured, candidate promotion uses matched-baseline non-regression plus explicit cost evidence.

Required principles:
- no additional always-on polling for visuals
- no global decorative canvas/WebGL/particle runtime by default
- no continuous hidden/offscreen animation
- Lite tier removes nonessential expensive effects
- reduced-motion path does not keep invisible animation work alive
- large visual assets are lazy unless required for first interaction
- candidate asset/CSS/DOM growth is recorded
- any material startup or interaction regression requires explicit tradeoff review and cannot be hidden by perceived-speed tricks

For important animations, frame throughput/jank evidence must be collected on representative execution environments before release-level claims.

## 8. Expert council

Major visual promotions require reviews through these lenses:
- Product Design
- Interaction Design
- Information Architecture
- Brand Identity
- Typography
- Motion Design
- Accessibility
- Arabic/RTL Localization
- Android Platform Fit
- Mobile Performance
- Simplifier

High-impact subjective judgments should use more than one structurally different route where practical. Evaluator identity/version belongs in the evidence ledger.

## 9. Candidate tournament

For a material redesign, generate a portfolio rather than one answer.

Minimum portfolio normally includes:
- baseline control
- conservative refinement
- simplifier candidate
- distinctive candidate
- one structurally different composition where appropriate

Additional candidates are justified by uncertainty, not by a quota.

Tournament order:
1. baseline/epoch identity check
2. C0 invariant gate
3. structural/accessibility gate
4. functional replay
5. RTL/localization gate
6. mobile/performance gate
7. screenshot/frame comparison
8. visual council
9. simplifier attack
10. anti-imitation check
11. holdout scenarios
12. Pareto decision

## 10. Promotion rules

Allowed outcomes:
- PROMOTE
- REJECT
- TRADEOFF_REQUIRES_DECISION
- INCONCLUSIVE
- ENVIRONMENT_INVALID
- MEASUREMENT_INVALID

PROMOTE requires:
- zero C0 failure
- zero unresolved material C1 regression
- exact baseline/candidate scenario binding
- evidence provenance
- no evaluator/benchmark change inside the comparison epoch
- objective improvements proven by objective evidence where possible
- subjective improvement supported by explicit independent judgment rather than stated as fact
- regression-cemetery cases remain passing
- rollback/revert path exists

A candidate may win without improving every axis only if no protected axis materially regresses and the Pareto trade is explicit.

## 11. Baseline/golden policy

Screenshots are anchors, not authority.

A pixel difference is not automatically a regression. A pixel match is not proof of correctness.

Every visual diff must be classified as one of:
- EXPECTED_IMPROVEMENT
- EXPECTED_NEUTRAL_CHANGE
- MATERIAL_REGRESSION
- INCONCLUSIVE
- BASELINE_STALE

Golden updates require a reason and evidence. A candidate may be correct while the old golden is wrong.

## 12. Anti-Goodhart controls

- keep public/dev/shadow/holdout visual cases
- rotate some content strings and long-content cases
- preserve hidden/late-stage scenarios for major visual freezes
- blind candidate order for subjective comparison where practical
- record judge identity/version
- do not optimize to one screenshot dimension
- do not reward novelty by itself
- do not reward similarity to another product by itself
- keep objective metrics separate from subjective preference

## 13. Comparison epoch

VISUAL-EPOCH-1 is invalidated and replaced when any material item changes:
- protected requirements
- scenario definitions used for decisive comparison
- judge/evaluator identity used for decisive comparison
- rendering environment in a way that can change outcome
- accessibility/performance threshold
- Design Genome objective

Historical evidence remains visible but cannot silently authorize promotion in the new epoch.

## 14. Saturation

The visual architecture/design campaign freezes only after two independent consecutive challenges under the same fixed constitution find NO MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT.

Any MATERIAL improvement resets the saturation counter to 0/2.

Saturation means no material architectural/design improvement was found within the documented search/evidence envelope. It does not mean universally perfect aesthetics.

## 15. Truth boundary

This constitution is a pre-candidate decision contract. It does not claim the current Seven UI passes every new target. Phase 0 explicitly found open gaps. Those gaps become work, not retroactive failures hidden from the record.
