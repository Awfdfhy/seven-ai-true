# Seven Polishing Protocol V4.3 — Maximum Research Loop

Status: CANDIDATE — successor to V4.2 Motion Evidence & Expert Judgment

## Purpose
Increase research recall and adversarial coverage as far as practical without pretending the internet is finite, fully indexed, stable, or exhaustively searchable. Then use The Loop to recursively challenge and improve the research process itself.

## Prime law
For a material decision, research breadth is bounded by evidence coverage and marginal information gain, not by a tiny fixed query count. The campaign expands search until applicable mandatory evidence surfaces are covered and additional diverse search has low expected material information gain relative to cost.

`MAXIMUM_RESEARCH != UNLIMITED_RESEARCH`.

## Wide Research Mesh
A material campaign builds a SearchSurfaceMap across applicable lanes:
1. primary standards/specifications;
2. platform/vendor documentation and changelogs;
3. peer-reviewed research and systematic reviews;
4. conference/workshop proceedings and preprints when relevant;
5. mature open-source implementations and issue histories;
6. production engineering reports/postmortems;
7. benchmarks/evaluation methodology;
8. accessibility/localization/internationalization;
9. security/privacy/authority guidance;
10. mobile/resource/performance evidence;
11. competitive/product pattern observation;
12. negative evidence, failures and counterexamples;
13. historical/legacy evidence when migrations or compatibility matter;
14. adjacent-domain analogies explicitly labeled analogous;
15. dissenting/contradictory evidence.

Search lanes are tailored by relevance. Irrelevant lanes are N/A with rationale rather than searched ceremonially.

## Query Portfolio
Never rely on one wording. Material research uses a portfolio when applicable:
- exact terminology;
- synonyms/older/newer terminology;
- problem-first queries;
- failure/counterexample queries;
- alternative-architecture queries;
- benchmark/evaluation queries;
- implementation/postmortem queries;
- accessibility/localization queries;
- resource/mobile queries;
- date/version-sensitive queries.

## Source Diversity and Seed Diversity
Seed evidence should vary across author/organization, year/version, venue/source class and viewpoint when practical. A famous source does not terminate discovery. Duplicate claims are clustered so ten copies of one idea do not masquerade as ten independent confirmations.

## Hybrid Discovery
Use direct/database/web discovery plus backward/forward citation or dependency snowballing where tools/evidence permit. Follow important references from high-value seed sources. Search newly discovered terminology. Repeat until marginal relevant discovery falls below the campaign's stop threshold or tooling/access boundaries prevent further search.

## Coverage Ledger
Every material research campaign records:
- searched lanes;
- query families;
- source classes;
- date/version range;
- languages searched where relevant;
- exclusions/access/tool limitations;
- duplicate clusters;
- contradictory evidence;
- unresolved gaps;
- last material discovery round;
- saturation/stop rationale.

Coverage is a bounded claim about this ledger, never 'the whole internet'.

## Recall-First, Quality-Second Funnel
Early discovery favors recall. Screening then ranks evidence by directness, authority, methodological quality, freshness, reproducibility and applicability to Seven. Weak sources may reveal terminology or failure modes but cannot silently become strong evidence.

## Evidence Triangulation
For high-impact decisions, seek multiple independent evidence classes when feasible. Primary specifications define contracts; research can test broader hypotheses; implementation evidence establishes feasibility; benchmarks establish measurable behavior; user/UX evidence informs human outcomes. Repetition within one class is not full triangulation.

## Contradiction Engine
Conflicts are first-class:
- preserve both claims and exact sources;
- identify scope/version/population/environment differences;
- search specifically for resolving evidence;
- lower confidence if conflict remains;
- never resolve by source popularity alone.

## Research Adversary
Before accepting a major decision, issue an adversarial search packet:
- evidence the candidate is wrong;
- superior alternatives;
- failure cases;
- hidden costs;
- conditions where benchmark reverses;
- platform/version incompatibility;
- accessibility/localization counterexamples;
- low-end mobile counterexamples.

A material discovery reopens the candidate and resets saturation.

## Freshness Mesh
Each material evidence item is STABLE, VERSION_BOUND or TIME_SENSITIVE. Time-sensitive decisions trigger fresh search during revalidation. Changelogs/release notes may be searched for version-bound assumptions. Freshness does not automatically make evidence better; it prevents stale assumptions from pretending to be current.

## Multilingual/Regional Search
When the domain is materially language/region dependent, expand beyond English, especially Arabic/RTL/localization evidence for Seven. Machine-translated understanding remains derived and uncertainty is preserved.

## Research Benchmark
Research itself is benchmarkable where a reference set or retrospective evaluation exists. Track diagnostics such as:
- known-relevant-source recovery;
- unique material discoveries per search round;
- evidence-class coverage;
- duplicate rate;
- contradiction discovery;
- stale-evidence detection;
- counterexample yield;
- time/cost per material discovery.

These diagnostics do not collapse into one universal score.

## Search Holdout
For protocol self-evaluation, reserve hidden/late search seeds, terminology or known counterexamples when practical. A successor that merely optimizes the visible query portfolio does not prove stronger discovery.

## The Recursive Research Loop
1. lock parent protocol and research benchmark;
2. run parent research process against a target corpus/problem;
3. inspect misses, blind spots, stale assumptions and wasted search;
4. propose successor search strategy;
5. compare on locked visible + holdout evidence where feasible;
6. integrate only material improvements;
7. promote discovered misses to regression cases;
8. adversarially search for a better search strategy;
9. reset 0/2 after material improvement;
10. freeze only after V4 eligibility and two independent clean challenges.

The loop does not recurse infinitely. It stops when mandatory coverage is satisfied and additional search/process complexity fails to produce material information gain.

## Anti-Research-Bloat Governor
Research depth scales with decision criticality, uncertainty, reversibility and evidence freshness. Cheap/reversible C3 decisions do not receive release-critical systematic-review effort. C0/C1 and architecture-wide decisions may escalate to the widest practical mesh.

## Anti-SEO / Source Laundering
Search ranking is discovery metadata, not evidence authority. Prefer original/primary sources when available. Secondary pages may route to primary evidence. Repeated derivative articles do not increase confidence independently.

## Tool/Access Honesty
Record inaccessible databases, unavailable full text, indexing limits, robots/access restrictions, unavailable citation graphs and search-tool caps. Never translate inability to search a surface into evidence that no relevant evidence exists.

## UI/Motion specialization
For visual/motion campaigns, Wide Research Mesh also searches:
- current platform motion guidance;
- HCI/perception/accessibility evidence;
- animation/runtime performance guidance;
- high-quality product patterns across multiple design families;
- failure examples and anti-patterns;
- Arabic/RTL motion and typography evidence where available.
Taste observations remain hypotheses until supported by appropriate user or multi-review evidence.

## Relationship to Benchmark/Visual/Motion planes
Research discovers what to test and which failure surfaces matter. Benchmarks measure. Visual/video evidence observes rendered behavior. Expert Council judges multi-axis product quality. None can silently replace another.

## Canonical adoption rule
V4.3 may supersede V4.2 only after self-polish under The Loop, research-process benchmark/holdout where feasible, adversarial coverage review, simplifier attack and two independent clean challenges. Until then the previous canonical protocol remains fallback.
