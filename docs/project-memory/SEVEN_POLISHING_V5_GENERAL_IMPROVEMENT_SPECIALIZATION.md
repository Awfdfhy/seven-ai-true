# Seven Polishing V5 — General Improvement Specialization

Status: ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE
Parent substrate: Seven General Improvement Engine 1.0
Historical parents inherited: V4.4 Velocity Fabric, V4.3 Maximum Research Loop, V4.2 Motion & Expert Judgment, V4.1 Benchmark/Visual Loop, V4 safety laws.

## Purpose
Seven Polishing V5 is no longer a separate optimizer. It is a specialization layer over the General Improvement Engine. The general engine provides recursive improvement mechanics, candidate tournaments, hostility escalation, evidence lineage, attribution, evaluator calibration, transfer checks, improvement economics and governance separation. Polishing adds domain-discovery, aesthetic/expert judgment, benchmark families, visual/motion evidence, independent saturation challenges and freeze semantics.

## Core rule
Polishing may optimize anything that is inside the declared ImprovementTarget, but may not redefine the target's authority, success criteria, hidden holdouts, C0/C1 gates or Promotion Gate after evidence is observed.

## Campaign flow
1. Lock target identity, requirements, invariants and evidence ladder.
2. Discover full relevant domain surface and unknown regions.
3. Build utility tree and non-compensable constraints.
4. Run Maximum Research Mesh proportional to materiality.
5. Generate diverse candidate families, including simplification/removal candidates.
6. Build causal hypotheses for each proposed improvement.
7. Precommit evaluation package and hostility profile.
8. Run cheap deterministic rejection gates first.
9. Run targeted benchmark/visual/motion/expert lanes as applicable.
10. Run attribution and transfer checks.
11. Run adversarial counterexample and contradiction search.
12. Use Debugging & Repair Fabric for candidate defects without laundering failures.
13. Run hidden/holdout evaluation.
14. Evaluate complexity, mobile cost, battery, RAM, storage, maintenance and coupling.
15. Independent Judge verdict.
16. Promotion Gate verdict.
17. If promoted, harden hostility and repeat up to the recursive campaign ceiling.
18. After no qualifying recursive evolution remains, enter bounded saturation review.
19. Require two independent consecutive NO_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT challenges with no unresolved critical failure.
20. Freeze only the bounded search/evidence/resource envelope actually demonstrated.

## 200% Materiality inside Polishing
A recursive generation is earned only by a step-change. Literal >=2x language is allowed only for valid ratio metrics with precommitted measurement. Non-ratio qualities such as correctness, safety, aesthetics, usability and architectural quality must use categorical capability jumps or strong multi-axis Pareto dominance. Smaller useful improvements remain ordinary polishing deltas and can be accumulated without consuming a recursive-generation slot.

## Escalating hostility
Each promoted recursive generation must harden at least one target-relevant axis without weakening inherited protections. Candidate examples:
- stronger unseen counterexamples
- broader edge-case families
- fresh holdouts
- distribution shifts
- process-death/recovery/interruption cases
- lower-memory or slower-device envelopes
- Arabic/RTL/accessibility/theme/reduced-motion variants
- stronger competitor or baseline candidates
- longer-horizon workflows
- evaluator mutation tests
- stronger reproducibility requirements
- fault injection where safe

Harder does not mean indiscriminately more tests. V4.4 may deduplicate, cache, impact-select and parallelize evidence when semantic coverage remains valid.

## Polishing-specific evidence planes
When relevant, V5 retains:
- deterministic structural checks
- benchmark suites with PUBLIC/DEVELOPMENT, SHADOW, HOLDOUT and RELEASE partitions
- visual evidence bound to build/state/device/locale/theme/tier
- motion evidence bound to runtime traces and recording context
- expert lenses for product design, motion, accessibility, performance, interaction, brand, localization, simplification and broad-audience hypotheses
- research evidence with source diversity, contradiction search, freshness and explicit inaccessible regions

Subjective findings remain labeled as subjective or hypothesis-level evidence. Visual/video evidence cannot claim runtime facts it cannot measure.

## Evaluator integrity
The evaluator is a first-class attack surface. Known-good, known-bad, deceptive and mutation-generated candidates are periodically injected. If Judge behavior is not calibrated enough to distinguish them, promotion authority is suspended until evaluator repair/revalidation.

## Attribution discipline
A candidate that improves after many simultaneous changes should, where practical, undergo ablation/differential checks. Gains may not be attributed to a component without evidence that separates its effect from confounders.

## Transfer discipline
A benchmark win is narrow until it survives adjacent unseen scenarios. Promotion claims explicitly distinguish benchmark-local, environment-bound and transferable improvement.

## Improvement economics
A candidate may be better in one quality axis but still lose promotion if the improvement requires disproportionate RAM, battery, storage, latency, cloud cost, maintenance burden or architectural coupling. No universal scalar hides this tradeoff; report Pareto movement and hard constraints.

## Recursive relation to Self-Evolution
Self-Evolution can invoke the General Improvement Engine and Polishing V5 as a quality-development service. It cannot inherit Promotion Gate authority from them. Candidate self-modification, benchmark modification and policy modification remain separated from external governance.

## Saturation law
Recursive evolution and polishing saturation are distinct:
- recursive evolution asks whether a qualifying step-change can be promoted;
- polishing saturation asks whether any remaining material improvement exists inside the documented search surface and resource envelope.

After recursive STOP, run two independent saturation challenges. Any MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND resets the count to zero.

## Truth boundary
This document defines a successor architecture candidate. It is not yet a runtime implementation and does not prove that V5 is empirically superior to V4.4. V4.4 remains the active proven governance protocol until V5 survives its own validation/freeze campaign.
