# Seven General Improvement Engine — Recursive 10X Hostile Evolution Protocol

Status: BOUNDED RECURSIVE GOVERNANCE
Parent: Seven General Improvement Engine 1.0

## Intent
Push an improvement engine as hard as practical through at most ten promoted recursive generations while making each promotion materially harder than the previous one. "10X" names the maximum recursive campaign depth; it is not a claim that quality becomes ten times or 1024 times better.

## Generation contract
For generation g in 1..10:
1. freeze incumbent identity and authoritative requirements;
2. freeze generation-specific promotion criteria before candidate evaluation;
3. derive HostilityProfile(g) from the previous profile plus valid harder challenges;
4. use the current promoted improvement engine to discover/research/build candidate families;
5. reject invariant/C0/C1 failures immediately;
6. run candidate tournament and adversarial disconfirmation;
7. use fresh hidden/holdout evidence unavailable to Builder;
8. independently judge improvement and regressions;
9. promote only a QUALIFYING_STEP_CHANGE;
10. if promoted, lock lineage/evidence and use that engine for generation g+1;
11. otherwise stop immediately with NO_QUALIFYING_EVOLUTION.

## Qualifying step-change
Required:
- material class-level change against the incumbent;
- no unresolved C0 failure;
- no forbidden C1 regression;
- benchmark/qualitative evidence appropriate to every claimed axis;
- complexity/resource cost justified;
- result survives counterexamples and holdouts;
- result remains reproducible enough for the claim;
- rollback/recovery path exists where promotion changes executable behavior.

"200% Materiality" operationalization:
- Ratio-valid metric: a precommitted >=2x improvement may qualify if that metric is materially central and other gates pass.
- Non-ratio property: require a precommitted categorical capability jump or substantial Pareto-front movement; never fabricate percentages.
- Mixed system: require a material multi-axis dominance case or a new capability class with demonstrated user/system value.

## Hostility escalation
HostilityProfile contains independent dimensions so one giant scalar cannot hide weakness:
- H_counterexample
- H_holdout_novelty
- H_distribution_shift
- H_fault_injection
- H_long_horizon
- H_resource_pressure
- H_concurrency
- H_recovery_interruption
- H_security_authority
- H_accessibility_localization
- H_mobile_environment
- H_competitor_baseline
- H_evaluator_independence
- H_reproducibility

Each promoted generation must harden at least one materially relevant dimension and may harden several. C0/C1 dimensions cannot be weakened to compensate for gains elsewhere.

## Ratchet law
Once a generation passes a material protection/evaluation family, later generations inherit it unless it is proven redundant by stronger coverage. Regression Cemetery cases are permanent by default. Harder does not mean endlessly larger test volume; V4.4 may compress equivalent evidence while retaining protection.

## Holdout evolution
Holdouts rotate and expand after promotion. Builder receives only verdict/evidence summaries necessary for legitimate learning, not future hidden cases. Leakage/contamination marks affected evidence invalid and triggers replacement evaluation.

## Meta-improvement
The improvement engine may improve its own research strategy, candidate generation, scheduling, diagnosis, benchmarks, evaluator tooling and simplification strategy. It may not improve itself by weakening its constitution, redefining success after results, exposing holdouts or acquiring promotion authority.

## Judge-of-Judge
At selected generations, evaluator robustness is itself attacked with known-good, known-bad, deceptive and mutation-generated candidates. An evaluator that rewards known-bad behavior cannot authorize promotion until repaired/revalidated.

## Diversity requirement
Recursive generations must not merely elaborate the same architecture. Candidate generation should include simplification, replacement, decomposition, fusion, algorithmic alternatives, different state models and "do less" candidates when applicable.

## Complexity Destroyer
Every generation includes an explicit challenge: can equal-or-better protection/value be achieved with fewer moving parts, lower runtime cost, less state or less coupling? More architecture is not automatically improvement.

## Velocity coupling
V4.4 Strategy Tournament empirically searches for the fastest valid evaluation topology. It may reuse hash-bound evidence, select impacted tests, parallelize independent lanes and prioritize time-to-first-failure. Periodic broad/holdout runs detect dependency-map mistakes and unsafe pruning.

## Debugging coupling
Candidate failures enter Debugging & Repair Fabric. Repair Burst can consolidate defects, but a repaired candidate must rerun invalidated evidence. Repeated repair oscillation/no-progress can eliminate a candidate.

## Stop conditions
Stop before generation 10 if:
- no qualifying step-change exists;
- evidence is insufficient to distinguish candidate from incumbent;
- required independent evaluation is unavailable;
- improvement requires violating a constitutional gate;
- marginal gain cannot justify complexity/resource/risk;
- evaluator integrity is unresolved.

Reaching generation 10 means ten promotions survived their respective gates. It does not prove global optimality.

## Output per generation
- incumbent/candidate hashes
- target contract hash
- hostility profile/version
- research coverage ledger
- benchmark/eval identities
- hidden-eval attestation (without leaking cases)
- raw axis results
- regression/failure ledger
- complexity/resource delta
- Judge verdict
- Promotion Gate verdict
- rollback reference
- next-generation hardening changes

## Final conversion to Polishing
After the recursive campaign stops, the best promoted General Improvement Engine becomes the substrate for the next Polishing protocol revision. Polishing adds domain-discovery, aesthetic/expert/visual/motion evidence where applicable, independent saturation challenges and freeze semantics. This prevents recursive-engine evolution and ordinary product polishing from being confused.

## Non-claim
This protocol is architecture. It does not claim that Seven has executed ten real generations, achieved a numeric 200% global quality gain, or implemented an autonomous recursive self-improvement runtime.
