## SEVEN MAXIMUM EFFORT — TOOL FABRIC DISCOVERY COVERAGE AUDIT 03

Goal: determine whether Seven Tool Fabric 2.0 broad discovery has reached practical saturation after Waves 01–19. The audit must prefer closing discovery and beginning deep engineering once all material P0/P1 capability classes are covered, but it must not hide a real gap merely to finish the phase.

Canonical inputs:
- `SEVEN_CAPABILITIES_MAP.md`
- `SEVEN_MASTER_PLAN.md`
- `SEVEN_TOOL_FABRIC_2_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`
- Coverage Audits 01 and 02
- Waves 01–19 reports/commands/evidence
- current Deep Polish directory/state

Audit method:
1. remap all current Seven capability classes to researched architecture/candidates;
2. explicitly test the Wave 19 generative-media gap closure;
3. search conceptually for remaining broad general-assistant P0/P1 classes;
4. separate product/architecture polish from missing Tool Fabric primitives;
5. classify remaining ideas as P2, specialist, future product expansion, or duplication;
6. assess security/authority/lineage/recovery/Android coverage across the whole tool plane;
7. identify candidate duplication and consolidation needed before implementation;
8. decide whether another broad wave has greater expected value than tool-by-tool Deep Polish;
9. produce a frozen transition queue if saturated.

Saturation definition:
`SATURATED_FOR_BROAD_DISCOVERY` means no material unresearched P0/P1 general capability class remains. It does not mean all providers/libraries are known, all tools are implemented, or future targeted research is forbidden.

Discovery may later reopen only for a concrete implementation gap, measured failure, important user/product requirement, or material new technology.

Required output:
1. final broad-capability coverage matrix
2. P0/P1 gap verdict
3. remaining P2/future/deferred areas
4. duplication/consolidation findings
5. broad-discovery freeze decision
6. ordered Deep Polish transition plan
7. reopen conditions
