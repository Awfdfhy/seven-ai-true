# Seven Self-Evolution Engine 4.0 — Constitutional Recursive Development Fabric

Status: ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE
Parent: Self-Evolution + General Improvement Integration
Governance: Seven Polishing V5.40 Meta-Assurance Closure

## Purpose
Turn Seven self-development into a governed engineering system that can discover weaknesses, generate genuinely different improvements, learn from evidence, build or revise implementation artifacts, and recursively improve its own development methods without controlling the constitution that decides whether those changes are valid.

## Prime laws
1. Opportunity is not permission.
2. Candidate generation is not promotion.
3. Self-evaluation is evidence, never authority.
4. A promoted generation may improve the next Builder, but cannot inherit control of Judge, Promotion, hidden evidence, or target authority.
5. Production Seven does not silently rewrite its installed application. Self-development produces versioned candidate artifacts that cross the normal release boundary.
6. Improvement claims are bounded by V5.40: requirement fidelity, goal fidelity, benchmark health, evidence resolution, environment identity, live/replay distinction, and claim scope.

## 1. Four evolution lanes
### Lane A — Runtime Adaptation
Low-authority, reversible adaptation such as experience retrieval, routing preferences, cache policy, non-authoritative procedural hints, and local personalization. It cannot rewrite canonical requirements, permissions, release code, or evaluator constitution.

### Lane B — Engineering Evolution
Creates proposed changes to architecture, algorithms, prompts, tools, workflows, tests, UI/UX, performance strategies, provider integration, memory/context logic, and code. Changes remain candidate artifacts until independently evaluated and promoted.

### Lane C — Meta-Evolution
Improves the development machinery itself: research strategy, candidate generation, scheduling, debugging, experience compilation, benchmark construction, evaluator tooling, and simplification. Meta-Evolution receives stricter independence requirements because it changes the machinery that creates future candidates.

### Lane D — Model/Training Evolution
Parameter training or weight modification is a distinct capability class and is not implicitly enabled by Self-Evolution 4.0. If introduced later, it requires a separate training/data/evaluation authority contract.

## 2. Protected Evolution Kernel
The following are outside ordinary candidate authority:
- authoritative product goal and user-directed strategic priorities;
- Authority/Permission semantics;
- Constitution Store and V5.40 promotion rules;
- hidden-evidence boundary;
- Promotion Gate authority;
- Evidence Ledger integrity rules;
- rollback/recovery requirement;
- release boundary;
- canonical lineage history.

A candidate may propose changes to these only through a separate governance campaign. It may never make such a proposal effective by evaluating or approving itself.

## 3. ImprovementTarget 4.0
Each campaign starts with a hash-bound target contract containing:
- target identity/version;
- Goal Receipt: intended user/system outcome and explicit non-goals;
- requirement-to-evidence map;
- authoritative invariants and forbidden regressions;
- affected capabilities and dependencies;
- criticality class;
- expected improvement mechanism;
- optimization axes without collapsing them into one universal score;
- resource/mobile envelope;
- environment and horizon envelope;
- observability and evidence-resolution requirements;
- benchmark identities and health requirements;
- live/replay evidence needs;
- rollback/migration contract;
- maximum campaign budget;
- stop conditions;
- unresolved assumptions and unknown regions.

## 4. Opportunity Discovery Plane
Signals may come from:
- user feedback and explicitly authorized goals;
- evaluation failures;
- regression cemetery recurrence;
- runtime/device evidence;
- debugging/repair findings;
- research updates;
- provider/platform drift;
- performance/resource evidence;
- repeated manual work or maintenance cost;
- benchmark/coverage gaps.

Opportunity Discovery can rank and explain opportunities. It cannot redefine Seven's strategic goal. Strategic direction remains governed by the authoritative goal contract.

## 5. Candidate Portfolio Engine
For every material target, generation should search across structurally different candidate families where relevant:
- add a mechanism;
- remove a mechanism;
- simplify;
- replace;
- decompose;
- fuse;
- reroute;
- localize;
- cache/reuse;
- change state representation;
- change retrieval/planning algorithm;
- build a tool;
- change evidence strategy;
- do nothing/incumbent baseline.

Every candidate carries a Mechanism Card: what changes, why it should help, what evidence would support the mechanism, likely regressions, dependencies, complexity delta, and predicted resource cost.

## 6. Generation Firebreak
For generation g:
- Builder_g may create candidate Builder_(g+1).
- Judge_g and Promotion_g remain outside Builder_(g+1)'s authority.
- hidden evidence for g remains inaccessible to Builder.
- future hidden evidence for g+1 remains inaccessible after promotion.
- campaign constitution hash is fixed before interpreting candidate results.

If a candidate modifies evaluator tooling, benchmark generation, or evidence processing, it cannot be the sole evaluator of its own update. The old evaluation path or a structurally independent path must bridge the change.

## 7. Evaluator Change Escrow
Evaluator changes use a separate escrow:
1. evaluate the new evaluator on known-good, known-bad, neutral, simplified, mutated, and historical-regression candidates;
2. compare old and new evaluator behavior under matched evidence;
3. preserve disagreement cases;
4. establish which claim classes the new evaluator is allowed to judge;
5. only after this bridge may later product candidates rely on it.

An evaluator improvement never retroactively upgrades old candidate evidence without revalidation.

## 8. Experience Compiler
Self-Evolution learns from released evidence without turning hidden tests into an answer key.

The compiler stores derived experience as typed records:
- FAILURE_PATTERN;
- PROCEDURAL_PRINCIPLE;
- MECHANISM_EVIDENCE;
- TOOL_USE_HEURISTIC;
- RESOURCE_LESSON;
- RECOVERY_LESSON;
- REJECTED_DESIGN_REASON.

Each record binds source lineage, scope, confidence/evidence state, applicable target class, freshness, and negative-transfer observations. Raw authoritative truth remains separate.

Detailed hidden cases are not exposed to Builder unless explicitly released from the hidden partition.

## 9. Experience Stability-Plasticity Guard
Experience memory is not assumed beneficial merely because it is larger.

Required controls:
- utility-aware retrieval;
- negative-transfer tracking;
- expiry/freshness where conditions change;
- duplicate/contradiction handling;
- procedural abstraction when detailed trajectories overfit;
- scope labels so local tricks do not become global rules;
- retrieval ablations against no-memory and simpler-memory baselines;
- forgetting audits for previously protected task families.

## 10. Capability Conservation Set
Every promoted generation inherits a versioned set of protected capabilities and regressions. Evaluation combines target-specific proof with sampled broad sentinels. A candidate cannot earn promotion by improving the target while silently degrading unrelated protected capabilities.

The conservation set evolves only through evidence-backed governance, not because a candidate makes a test inconvenient.

## 11. Learning From Rejection
Rejected candidates are first-class evidence. The system records why they failed, which mechanisms were disproven, where results were ambiguous, and whether the failure was candidate-specific or evaluator/environment-specific.

Future Builder generations receive bounded lessons, not hidden-case contents. This reduces repeated dead ends and survivorship bias from learning only from winners.

## 12. Tool and Artifact Evolution
Self-created tools, code, prompts, schemas, benchmarks, and workflow assets are versioned artifacts, never magical new capabilities.

A generated tool requires:
- capability contract;
- permission class;
- input/output schema;
- tests and negative cases;
- resource profile;
- version/hash;
- provenance;
- failure semantics;
- rollback/removal path;
- dependency list;
- retirement criteria.

A tool created by Builder cannot authorize itself or bypass Tool Fabric/Authority Kernel rules.

## 14. Promotion Escrow
Before canonical adoption, a candidate waits in a non-authoritative escrow with:
- candidate hash;
- evidence bundle;
- target/constitution hashes;
- migration plan;
- rollback reference;
- evidence debt;
- unresolved assumptions;
- required fresh checks;
- expected resource delta;
- release compatibility.

Escrow verdicts:
- PROMOTE_CANONICAL;
- PROMOTE_EXPERIMENTAL;
- SHADOW_ONLY;
- HOLD_FOR_EVIDENCE;
- REJECT_REGRESSION;
- REJECT_EVALUATOR_INVALID;
- REJECT_GOAL_MISMATCH;
- REJECT_COMPLEXITY_COST;
- STOP_NO_QUALIFYING_EVOLUTION.

## 15. Release Boundary
A successful Self-Evolution campaign produces an approved candidate artifact. It does not silently patch the installed Android application.

Executable release still passes the normal build, test, device, signing/distribution, migration, rollback, and release gates. Architecture acceptance and release proof remain different evidence states.

## 16. Recursive 10-generation campaign
Maximum promoted generations per recursive campaign remains 10; minimum 0.

Generation n+1 may use the promoted Builder improvements from generation n, but:
- Goal Receipt remains authoritative;
- Campaign Constitution remains externally governed;
- Judge/Promotion authority does not migrate into Builder;
- regression cemetery and conservation set ratchet forward;
- holdouts rotate;
- evidence exposure is tracked;
- hostility hardening must remain relevant rather than merely larger;
- each recursive slot requires a true step-change class, while smaller useful fixes may be accumulated without consuming a slot.

## 17. Frontier and Diversity Preservation
Recursive improvement risks converging on one architecture family. The engine therefore maintains:
- candidate-family diversity ledger;
- rejected-design archive;
- structural novelty budget;
- simplifier challenger;
- at least one materially different baseline when uncertainty remains;
- explicit detection of repeated local-optimum cycles.

Diversity is useful only when it creates distinct hypotheses or failure coverage; decorative variants do not count.

## 18. Direction-Setting Boundary
Self-Evolution may discover and rank opportunities, but it does not autonomously redefine what Seven should become. User/product authority defines strategic goals. The engine optimizes within that authorized direction and may propose a governance campaign when evidence suggests the goal contract itself needs revision.

## 19. Efficient Reflection and Research
Recursive reflection is not automatically valuable. V4.4 schedules the smallest valid evidence path. The engine prefers:
- early deterministic rejection;
- single-pass principle/procedure extraction when sufficient;
- evidence reuse with identity/freshness compatibility;
- stop-on-disproof;
- marginal-information stopping for research;
- parallel independent lanes only when contention does not distort evidence;
- periodic broad audits to detect selector mistakes.

More reflection cycles require measured value, not ritual repetition.

## 20. Self-Evolution health metrics
No universal self-improvement percentage exists. Track a vector including:
- valid promotion yield;
- time-to-disproof;
- evidence cost per valid promotion;
- regression escape rate;
- recurrence of known failure classes;
- transfer retention;
- negative-transfer rate;
- rollback/recovery success;
- candidate diversity;
- evaluator calibration health;
- benchmark health;
- claim calibration;
- resource delta on representative mobile tiers;
- maintenance/complexity delta;
- proportion of promotions later downgraded by fresher evidence.

## 21. Stop conditions
Stop or pause a campaign when:
- no qualifying candidate survives;
- Goal Receipt becomes ambiguous or stale;
- evaluator integrity is unresolved;
- evidence contamination invalidates the decision surface;
- candidate search collapses into repeated equivalent designs;
- negative transfer exceeds the accepted envelope;
- repair oscillation/no-progress persists;
- required rollback/recovery proof is unavailable;
- resource/evaluation budget is exhausted;
- environment drift invalidates the comparison;
- new evidence shows the target itself was mis-specified.

## 22. V5.40 inheritance
All Self-Evolution 4.0 campaigns inherit V5.40 Meta-Assurance Closure, including requirement fidelity, goal fidelity, benchmark health, evidence resolution, live/replay duality, claim-scope ceilings, observer/evaluation effects, contradiction preservation, simplification pressure, environment identity, and constitution-reset semantics.

Major revisions of Self-Evolution itself require two independent architecture saturation challenges before freeze.

## Truth boundary
This document is architecture. It does not claim Seven currently executes autonomous recursive campaigns, manages sealed holdouts, generates production-ready tools, changes its installed APK, performs model-weight training, or automatically promotes/releases evolved candidates.
