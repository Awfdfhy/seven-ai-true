# Seven Polishing V4.3 — Maximum Research Loop Self-Evolution Ledger

## Baseline
Parent: V4.2 Motion Evidence & Expert Judgment.
Candidate: V4.3 Maximum Research Loop.
Goal: maximize practical research recall/diversity and recursively improve the research process without unlimited-search claims or research bloat.

## External evidence used
The campaign reviewed current and established evidence on systematic mapping/search, hybrid database+snowballing strategies, seed-set diversity, representative evaluation sampling, Android benchmarking and qualitative-vs-quantitative evaluation.

Key implications:
- systematic mapping benefits from explicit database search, deduplication, snowballing, study selection, quality assessment and data extraction rather than superficial search alone;
- hybrid discovery can improve recall over a single discovery mode;
- diverse seed composition can improve snowballing coverage;
- representative sampling and iterative refinement are legitimate when exhaustive evaluation is impractical;
- benchmarks need controlled environments and do not replace qualitative evaluation.

## Loop Round 01 — Search breadth
Finding: V4.2 required evidence diversity but did not specify a sufficiently broad searchable surface.
Improvement: Wide Research Mesh with 15 tailored lanes and Coverage Ledger.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND. Reset 0/2.

## Round 02 — Query monoculture
Finding: broad source classes can still fail if every query uses the same vocabulary.
Improvement: Query Portfolio covering exact/synonym/problem/failure/alternative/benchmark/implementation/accessibility/resource/version-sensitive formulations.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 03 — Discovery topology
Finding: direct web/database queries alone can miss evidence discoverable from citations, references and newly learned terminology.
Improvement: Hybrid Discovery with backward/forward snowballing where tools permit and terminology expansion.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 04 — Popularity bias
Finding: many derivative sources can create false consensus.
Improvement: duplicate claim clustering, seed diversity, anti-SEO/source-laundering rule and primary-source preference.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 05 — Confirmation bias
Finding: breadth does not guarantee active search for disconfirmation.
Improvement: Research Adversary packet and Contradiction Engine.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 06 — Staleness
Finding: architecture can be grounded in once-correct but expired platform/provider evidence.
Improvement: Freshness Mesh and explicit revalidation of VERSION_BOUND/TIME_SENSITIVE evidence.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 07 — English-only blind spot
Finding: Seven has Arabic/RTL and regional concerns that English-only evidence can underrepresent.
Improvement: conditional multilingual/regional search with translation uncertainty preserved.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 08 — Can research quality be measured?
Finding: The Loop benchmarks products but could not detect a research process that confidently misses known evidence.
Improvement: Research Benchmark diagnostics plus holdout search seeds/counterexamples where feasible.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 09 — Recursive process improvement
Finding: research improvements themselves could overfit visible failures.
Improvement: Recursive Research Loop with locked parent, visible+holdout evidence, permanent miss regressions and adversarial search for superior search strategies.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 10 — Research explosion
Finding: maximizing breadth can become wasteful and slow every trivial decision.
Improvement: Anti-Research-Bloat Governor scales depth by criticality, uncertainty, reversibility and freshness; mandatory coverage plus marginal information gain controls stopping.
Verdict: MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_FOUND.

## Round 11 — Simplifier
Attack: replace the mesh with a fixed large number of queries/sources.
Result: rejected. A fixed count is simultaneously wasteful for narrow decisions and inadequate for broad/high-risk decisions. Coverage-based stopping is simpler semantically because it expresses the actual objective.
Attack: remove holdout research evaluation.
Result: rejected for protocol self-polish because visible-query optimization can masquerade as recall improvement.
Verdict: NO_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT.

## Independent Challenge A — Information retrieval / evidence synthesis lens
Examined: recall, seed diversity, hybrid discovery, snowballing, deduplication, contradictions, source quality, stop criteria, access limitations.
Potential improvement considered: require exhaustive enumeration of all web sources. Rejected as impossible and dishonest for an open, changing, partially inaccessible web.
Potential improvement considered: minimum universal source count. Rejected; source count is not coverage and encourages low-value duplication.
Verdict: NO_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT → 1/2.

## Independent Challenge B — Product/engineering efficiency lens
Examined: research latency, mobile/project constraints, freshness, benchmark integration, UI/motion specialization, Goodhart risk, operational cost.
Potential improvement considered: always run maximum-depth research. Rejected because it violates selective compute and adds latency without material value for reversible low-risk decisions.
Potential improvement considered: collapse research, benchmark and expert judgment into one score. Rejected because evidence types are non-substitutable and critical failures are non-compensable.
Verdict: NO_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT → 2/2.

## Saturation conclusion
V4.3 architecture reached bounded 2/2 after material improvements and applicable self-polish. This means no further material protocol improvement was demonstrated within the documented research/process surface. It does not mean the internet has been exhaustively searched or that the executable research benchmark is implemented.

## Reopen triggers
- demonstrated important source class/query family repeatedly missed;
- superior search/synthesis methodology;
- search-tool capability change;
- material benchmark showing lower recall than a simpler strategy;
- research cost exceeds value materially;
- systematic bias in language/region/source selection;
- evidence freshness failures;
- implementation evidence contradicts process assumptions.
