# Seven Polishing V5.40 — Meta-Assurance Closure

Status: ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE
Parent: V5.38 Benchmark Health

Purpose: close the remaining gap between evidence being present and evidence actually being trustworthy enough to support a promotion claim.

## Core closure laws
- requirement fidelity: evaluation rules must map to the intended requirement;
- goal fidelity: a metric win cannot outrank contrary product/system outcomes;
- benchmark health: measurement instruments must retain useful discrimination and freshness;
- evidence resolution: detection, localization, attribution, and reproduction are different evidence levels;
- live/replay duality: reproducibility and deployment realism remain separate evidence lanes;
- claim scope: every verdict is bounded by the weakest relevant requirement, oracle, environment, and observation channel;
- unresolved contradictions remain visible and cap the verdict;
- evaluation awareness, instrumentation effects, and observer effects are treated as possible measurement distortions;
- high-criticality claims require structurally different evidence routes where practical;
- changing requirements, benchmark identity, or material environment starts a new comparison epoch;
- missing observations never become evidence of absence;
- simplification may remove governance only when protected coverage is demonstrably preserved;
- V4.4 scheduling can reduce redundant work but cannot remove required proof;
- saturation is architecture-local and does not imply runtime implementation or release readiness.

## Saturation entry condition
V5.40 may enter independent saturation challenges only after all open material gaps from V5.35–V5.39 are represented in the fixed constitution. Any new material change resets the count.

## Truth boundary
